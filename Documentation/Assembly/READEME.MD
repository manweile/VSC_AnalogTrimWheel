# Assembly
The [assembly instructions](https://makers-point.com/3d-prints/trim-wheel-pro/trim-wheel-pro-guide/) from the creator are pretty good.  

## Tips
- Trim the solder joints on the underside of your Pro Micro. The slot for the board is quite tight, you need the vertical clearance.
- Use needle nose pliers to place the M3 nuts in their pockets. Those nuts are really small, especially if you have big hands.
- You can use female sockets on the Arduino and male ended wires on the sensor instead of male pins and female ended wires.
- I also used shrink wrap on the three wire sensor wires to bundle them, for stability prior to connecting to the Arduino.
