# 3D Printing for Analog Trim Wheel
## Source
https://www.printables.com/model/709536-trim-wheel-for-flight-simulator-hall-effect-sensor

## Printers
Creality Hi Combo

## Filaments
PETG and PLA

## Slicer Software
Creality Print 7.0

## Instructions
No supports for all printed objects.  
This did require some orientation changes, depending on which part.  
Used 0.12 mm layer height for both filaments.  

### Base
Printed in black PLA and PETG.  
Oriented on bottom face.  

### Lid
Printed in black PLA and PETG.  
Oriented on back face.  
Creality Print kept giving unsupported cantilevers error when oriented on top face or 45 degrees face.  
PETG also give me grief with extraneous filaments, especially in the nut pockets.  
Required lowering print speeds (initial and general), max extruder temperature, and infill pattern (needed gyroid).  
This could all be result of using Creality printer with a generic PETG.  
The designer used a Prusa MK3S at 0.2mm layer height.  

### Wheel
I printed the wheel in PLA only because I had that in white.  
Printing in white allowed me to write (with marker) on the wheel.  
A further modification could be use the embossing feature in Creality Print, oriented on non-shaft face.  
The flat keyed shaft hole did a very small ridge in it (where the wheel transitions to axle) which I had to remove.  

### Tensioner
Printed in black PLA and PETG.  
Oriented on side, with nut pocket opening exposed.  
For PETG, required inner & outer brim.  
PETG also had plate adhesion issues - Creality Print gave "please apply glue before print" warning.  
I didn't and did have the print job become unstuck several times until I increased bed temperature to max.  

### Spacer
Printed in black PLA and PETG.  
For PETG, required inner & outer brim.  
PETG also had plate adhesion issues - Creality Print gave "please apply glue before print" warning.  
I didn't and did have the print job become unstuck several times until I increased bed temperature to max.  

### Clamp
I got the clamp from Creality Cloud, but I'm sure you can find something suitable elsewhere.  

Printed in both PETG & PLA, and in black and white.  
Totally your choice of filament and colour.  

2 different size options  
- 2.75 inch vise
- 3 inch vise

Make sure to print the vise WITHOUT supports!  
Screw hole will be plugged up if you do, and good chance will not be usable.  

## Overall Results
PLA was better than PETG.  
- No filaments in nut pockets
- Faster
- No plate adhesion issues
